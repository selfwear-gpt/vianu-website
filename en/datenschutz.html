1. Privacy at a glance

General information

The following notes give a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is all data with which you can be personally identified. For detailed information on data protection, please see our privacy notice below this text.

Data collection on this website

Who is responsible for data collection on this website?

Data on this website is processed by the website operator. You can find the operator's contact details in the section "Information on the controller" in this privacy notice.

How do we collect your data?

On the one hand, your data is collected when you give it to us. This can be, for example, data that you enter in a contact form.

Other data is collected automatically, or after your consent, by our IT systems when you visit the website. This is mainly technical data (for example, internet browser, operating system or time of the page view). This data is collected automatically as soon as you enter this website.

What do we use your data for?

Part of the data is collected to ensure that the website is provided without errors. Where contracts can be concluded or initiated through the website, the data you submit is also processed for contract offers, orders or other requests.

What rights do you have regarding your data?

You have the right at any time to receive, free of charge, information about the origin, recipients and purpose of your stored personal data. You also have the right to request the correction or deletion of this data. If you have given consent to data processing, you can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right, under certain circumstances, to request the restriction of the processing of your personal data. Furthermore, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

You can contact us at any time about this and about further questions on data protection.

4. What this site does and does not do

  • This site sets no cookies.
  • This site uses no analytics or tracking services.
  • What you enter in forms stays in your browser's memory, for this tab only, until you close it. That way we pre-fill forms on this site for you. Nothing is sent while this happens.
  • Fonts, scripts and images load from the site's own server. Loading the site makes no connection to third-party servers.
  • The name you type into the name insole stays in your browser and is not sent.
  • The buttons for copying a link or text use only the clipboard of your device.
  • Links that employees pass on to their employer contain the addition quelle=mitarbeitende. It contains no details about people, and the site does not evaluate it.
  • The contact form in the popup and the request path on the start page send your details only when you click "Send" or "Send request". They then go directly to HubSpot.
  • When you click a link to a study, you leave this site. The rules of the other site apply there.

5. Contact form

Contact form
The contact form in the popup is a form on this site and is filled in over five steps. Only when you click "Send" in the last step does your browser send your details to HubSpot: your message, type of work, company size, company, role, city, how you found out about us, first name, last name and email, together with the address and language of the page and the text of your consent. Optional fields you leave empty are not sent. The details go to VIANU and are stored at HubSpot. No consent to marketing emails is sent.
Request path
The request path in the section "Your shift" is a form on this site. Only when you click "Send request" does your browser send your details to HubSpot: hours per shift, type of work, number of employees, role, company, email, how you found out about us and your message, together with the address and language of the page and the text of your consent. The details go to VIANU and are stored at HubSpot. No consent to marketing emails is sent.
Processing of the pilot request
The purpose is handling your request. The recipient is VIANU. HubSpot processes the details on our behalf. The contracting party is HubSpot, Inc. (USA), and a data processing agreement is in place. The data is stored in a HubSpot data centre in the European Union (Germany). HubSpot uses further service providers. HubSpot, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We delete requests that do not lead to an order after 12 months.
